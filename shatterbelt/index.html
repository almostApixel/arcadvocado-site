Shatterbelt

Pilot a tiny mining ship through a shattering asteroid belt. Blast rocks that crack and split, scoop up the gems they spill and race your oxygen home. Spend it all on a sprawling upgrade tree, launch again stronger, and break the belt's final boss.

Wishlist on Steam Screenshots
How it plays

Blast. Grab. Upgrade. Again.

One more run

Every run banks its gems, however it ends, and there's always an upgrade in reach. Runs start under a minute and grow as your ship does.

Every upgrade matters

Over a hundred upgrades: double and triple lasers, an auto beam that chains, homing missiles, a dash, shields, mining drones and a mothership with modules of its own.

A belt that fights back

Fifteen waves across the Dust Ring, the Frost Shelf and the Void Rim. Volatile rocks chain-react, golden ones flee, enemy ships hunt your mothership, and three bosses wait: the Hive Rock, the Corsair Flagship and the Maw.

More after the credits

Past the Maw the belt goes on: Endless with its own ring of upgrades, the Descent to start over stronger, and Expeditions, a branching chart of belts with mods to buy on the way.

  • About two and a half hours to finish the campaign, then Endless for mastery
  • Full controller support and Steam Deck ready, with rebindable keys and buttons
  • Comfort options: auto-fire, aim assist, reduced flashing, screen-shake settings, a CRT filter
  • Crisp 640×360 pixel art, one colour per meaning, so the chaos stays readable
